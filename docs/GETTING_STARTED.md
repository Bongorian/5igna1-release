# Your first photograph

[Guides](README.md) · [日本語](GETTING_STARTED.ja.md)

## Take your first photo

1. Allow camera access, choose **PHOTO** and **JPG** at the top. Start with recommended resolution; enable location/audio only when wanted.
2. Expand **FAULT**, open **+** and select **ROW ERROR**. Open its sliders icon and try displacement, bands and loss while viewing a vertical edge or moving subject.
3. Review the image above and confirm with **✓**, or cancel with **×**. LEVEL on the capture screen changes overall strength immediately.
4. Press the central shutter or a volume key. JPG saves processed output; RAW uses a separate exposure and does not exactly match the display.
5. Open the bottom-left thumbnail to view your work. Pinch to zoom, tap to reset zoom, then swipe down or use × to return to capture.

## Try next

**Explore combinations:** Open + and shuffle inside the editor. Inspect the new chain/values in the image; ✓ keeps them and × discards them. Capture-screen shuffle rebuilds settings immediately, so use the editor when you want to try and cancel. Holding capture-screen shuffle preserves the chain/controls and changes only seeds. [Randomization and RESEED](SEEDS.md).

**Control time:** Turn LIVE ON, expand FAULT and try Pause/Resume. The fault state holds while the camera continues moving. Use the adjacent sliders icon to try speed/cycle changes, then ✓ to keep them. Device inputs are not required. Some faults move intrinsically with LIVE OFF. [Using LIVE](LIVE_FAULT.md).

**Receive settings through a photo:** Choose Open photo at the top of the viewer, select the original photo, inspect SIGNAL and choose Shoot with this photo’s settings. Opening alone applies nothing. Processing the photo itself as a source uses the separate TAP feature. [Viewing, exchanging settings and TAP](USAGE.md).

## Revisit the controls

Open **Settings → Quick start** for the twelve-step guide. It explains screen roles and real control locations, with isolated practice for LEVEL and format. The camera pauses; capture settings and work remain unchanged. Use Next/Back or Skip at any time.

Ordinary Settings save immediately; ×/Back does not cancel them. This differs from ✓/× in FAULT and LIVE editors. Hold PHOTO/VIDEO to open resolution settings directly.
