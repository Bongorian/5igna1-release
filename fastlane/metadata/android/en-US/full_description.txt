5igna1 is a glitch camera inspired by failures along the imaging pipeline, from the sensor to the display. Preview distortions, color shifts and missing data while framing photos and videos.

16 EFFECTS
SENSOR FAIL, EXPOSURE BAND, ROW SHIFT, LINE LOSS, BIT ROT, DATA SHIFT, CFA TEAR, CFA OFFSET, DEMOSAIC, CHROMA, SPECTRUM, CHROMA LOSS, CORRUPT, PACKET LOSS, VHS and TERMINAL. CLEAN captures without effects. Available effects depend on the capture mode; PACKET LOSS is video-only.

CHAINS AND LIVE FAULT
Combine effects in processing order. Adjust intensity, shape and placement, preview changes, then apply or cancel. LIVE FAULT varies parameters automatically, with controls for interval, occurrence rate, duration and chain size. It starts off and does not require device movement.

PHOTOS AND VIDEO
Save processed JPEGs, original RAW or processed DNG on supported cameras, and GPU-processed videos. Select H.264 or H.265, resolution, frame rate and quality according to camera and encoder capabilities. Audio recording is optional.

EXPERIMENTAL RAW VIDEO
Compatible cameras can save original, silent DNG frames in a ZIP with timestamps. Extract it and develop the sequence in RAW software; it is not a directly playable MP4. Effects and LIVE FAULT are not applied. Frame rate depends on the device and storage speed; frames may be dropped under load.

ON YOUR DEVICE
No ads, accounts, trackers or analytics SDKs. Captures are processed and saved locally. Optional location tagging starts off. The same core features are available in every distribution, with no paid feature gates.

Japanese, English and Simplified Chinese interfaces. The default follows your device language. Requires Android 12 or later and a Camera2-compatible camera. RAW, resolutions and processing speed vary by device. RAW previews and DEMOSAIC are RGB approximations and can differ from developed sensor RAW.
